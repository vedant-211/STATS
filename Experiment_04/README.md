# Experiment 04: Statistical Inference

## Aim
To use sample statistics to draw inferences about the population using estimation and testing.

## Point Estimation & Confidence Intervals
| Parameter | Estimate | 95% Confidence Interval |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Mean Glucose | 121.66 | (119.50, 123.81) |
| Prop. Diabetic | 0.3490 | (0.3152, 0.3827) |

## Hypothesis Testing Results
| Test | H0 | Statistic | p-value | Decision |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| One-sample t | Mean Glucose = 120 | 1.5079 | 0.1320 | Fail to Reject H0 |
| Two-sample t | Mean BMI Group 0 = Group 1 | 9.0901 | 0.0000 | Reject H0 |
| Chi-Square | Age and Outcome Independent | 44.9450 | 0.0000 | Reject H0 |

## Student Questions & Answers
1. **What is the significance level?** We used alpha = 0.05.
2. **Is BMI significantly different?** Yes, the p-value is extremely low (0.0000), indicating a significant difference.
3. **Is Age associated with Diabetes?** Yes, the Chi-square test indicates a significant association.
